# SCEMATICA DYNAMICS — SPECIMEN FILE H-0
## Designation: "HILO"

> **CLASSIFICATION:** Anomalous energy-combatant
> **THREAT TIER:** S (area-denial class)
> **DISPOSITION:** Active, allied to S-1L
> **VISUAL TAG:** Slim silhouette, integrated arm-cannon, psionic-violet effects

---

### PHYSICAL PROFILE
Agile, acrobatic, built for spacing and control rather than brawling. Keeps
distance, dictates the lane, punishes anyone who closes it.

### COMBAT PROFILE — OBSERVED ABILITIES
- **Arm-cannon beams.** Fires sustained energy beams rather than discrete rounds.
  Beams **pierce**, **re-hit on an interval** (multi-tick), and carry hard
  pushback and hitstun across long range.
- **Sticky beam quality.** Beams can pin a struck target in the beam path,
  locking it in place while damage stacks.
- **Elemental beam types.** Observed in standard psionic, nuclear (radiation
  status), ice (freeze), and incendiary (burn) variants.
- **Claw melee.** Close-range physical strings and air combos for anything that
  slips inside beam range; aerial kicks, dive kicks, spin finishers.
- **Psionic state.** A heightened mode emphasizing floaty, free-moving, prolonged
  hover and psionic shock-waves with their own hitstun.
- **Mobility.** Dash/blink, sprint, air strings, hover.

### EQUIPMENT
Integrated arm-cannon; no external sidearm required — the weapon is the limb.

### FIELD NOTES
Cold, precise, mission-driven. Fights like someone who has already calculated the
exchange and decided it favours them. Pairs with S-1L as ranged control to his
pressure.

### CONTAINMENT RECOMMENDATION
Never allow open ground. The subject's entire kit converts distance into a kill
box. Force close quarters or do not engage.
