# SCEMATICA DYNAMICS — SPECIMEN FILE L-V
## Designation: "BIG MAN" LUCAS VEGAS

> **CLASSIFICATION:** Armed technician / loadout combatant
> **THREAT TIER:** A+ (escalates to S in heightened state)
> **DISPOSITION:** Active, allied to S-1L
> **VISUAL TAG:** Heavy-set gunfighter, multiple holstered weapons, gadget rig

---

### PHYSICAL PROFILE
Broad, grounded, deceptively quick. Fights like an engineer — every tool used
for exactly the job it was built for, nothing improvised that wasn't planned.

### COMBAT PROFILE — OBSERVED ABILITIES
- **Multi-weapon loadout.** Carries several distinct firearms, each with its own
  ammunition counter that **persists between switches** but **reloads on swap** —
  rewarding fast rotation between weapons rather than emptying one.
- **Per-weapon firing profiles.** Each weapon shows its own fire rate, projectile
  count, and spread (single, burst, scatter, rapid).
- **Dual-wield fire.** Can run two weapons at once, with muzzle flare resolving
  inside the firing arc.
- **Gadgets (four).** A **gravity** device, a **sound** device, a **metal-shard
  fragmentation** bomb, and an **electric** bomb — control and area tools layered
  over the gunplay.
- **"Big Man" state.** A heightened mode: lightning-fast movement, extended dash
  distance, and markedly more explosive impact effects.
- **Mobility.** Dash, sprint, air fire, gadget-assisted repositioning.

### EQUIPMENT
A walking armory: assault, shotgun, rail, SMG, and paired sidearms, plus the
four-gadget rig.

### FIELD NOTES
Talks more than the other two combined; means about half of it. Treats a
firefight as a problem with a known optimal solution and then executes it.

### CONTAINMENT RECOMMENDATION
Deny resupply and deny space. If the subject reaches heightened state, disengage —
standard cover does not survive the explosive uptick.
