# SCEMATICA DYNAMICS — SPECIMEN FILE VI
## Designation: "SIX"

> **CLASSIFICATION:** Anomalous bladed operative / clan founder
> **THREAT TIER:** S (single-operator strike class)
> **DISPOSITION:** Active; founder of the Black Moon Six (a.k.a. the Dark Moon Six)
> **VISUAL TAG:** Female shinobi profile, dark wrappings, oversized sheathed blade

---

### PHYSICAL PROFILE
Compact, controlled, eerily still until she isn't. Carries a blade that looks too
heavy for her frame and moves it as if it weighs nothing. Observers consistently
underestimate her on sight; observers are consistently wrong.

### COMBAT PROFILE — OBSERVED ABILITIES
- **Superhuman swordsmanship.** A complete, brutal sword art layered over hidden
  ninpo. Strikes land faster and heavier than her stature should allow.
- **Vastly superhuman strength and speed.** Swings a massive blade at a velocity
  that blurs the strokes together into a whirling storm of cuts.
- **Ki-based ninpo.** Projects and *extends* her own ki, landing it through her
  fists — physical strength fused with ki-strength so a single punch hits far
  past its apparent mass.
- **Jutsu.** Confirmed use of **summoning** and **substitution** techniques —
  appearing, vanishing, and trading place mid-engagement.
- **Future-tech ninja kit.** Homing micro-shuriken drones, programmable smoke,
  gravity-tipped kunai — old shinobi ideas rebuilt out of tomorrow.

### EQUIPMENT
A signature gravity-edged blade (a **gravity-ripper** weapon that parts armour
and stone alike), plus a full kit of advanced shinobi gear well beyond fielded
standard.

### FIELD NOTES
Speaks little, reveals less. Whatever the full extent of her ability is, field
observation has never seen the bottom of it — which is itself the most alarming
line in this file.

### CONTAINMENT RECOMMENDATION
Assume every observed capability is a fraction of the real one. Do not attempt
solo containment under any circumstances.
