# SCEMATICA DYNAMICS — SPECIMEN REGISTRY: "YIELDS"
## The Bounty Bestiary

*Internally, the creatures the public calls monsters are not called monsters.
They are called **yields.** Each is a bounty, a branding, and a unit of fear the
company converts into SCEMA. The street gets a myth; the ledger gets a number.*

---

### K-9 CLASS — "WEREWOLF"
> THREAT TIER: B–A · ROLE: aggressive bruiser
Fast, ferocious, lunging melee predator. The flagship myth — outperforms every
other branding for driving bounty-insurance sales. Hits hard, closes fast,
staggers under sustained pressure.

### TITAN CLASS
> THREAT TIER: A–S · ROLE: heavy / anchor
Enormous, armoured, slow. Tremendous health and knockback resistance; shrugs off
light fire and answers with ground-shaking blows. Canonical height far above hero
scale. Patience and positioning beat it; trading does not.

### CHIMERA CLASS
> THREAT TIER: A–S · ROLE: erratic apex
The largest and most unstable of the standard yields. Mixed-profile attacker —
unpredictable patterns, multiple threat vectors. The yield the company shows when
it wants a district truly afraid.

### DRONE CLASS
> THREAT TIER: C–B · ROLE: ranged harasser
Small, metallic, expendable. Sparks rather than bleeds (metal material). Fielded
in numbers to control space and punish the careless from range.

### HUMANOID UNITS
> THREAT TIER: C–A · ROLE: rank-and-file
Hero-scaled human and augmented combatants — escorts, enforcers, guards. Bleed
(flesh material), come armed and in groups, and fill out the contracts the larger
yields headline.

---

### MATERIAL NOTE
For the Bloody Combat Engine: **flesh** yields spray blood on impact; **metal**
yields throw sparks. The distinction drives both the visuals and the appropriate
ammunition response.
