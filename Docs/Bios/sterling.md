# SCEMATICA DYNAMICS — SPECIMEN FILE ARG-?
## Designation: UNIDENTIFIED — "THE SILVER SWORDSMAN"

> **CLASSIFICATION:** Unknown / unclassifiable
> **THREAT TIER:** S+ (no successful engagement on record)
> **DISPOSITION:** Active; observed operating alongside the Dark Moon Six
> **VISUAL TAG:** Long flowing silver hair, silver goatee and fine moustache,
> single very long blade

---

### PHYSICAL PROFILE
Tall, composed, unhurried. Carries himself with an old-fashioned, gentlemanly
calm and almost never speaks. Every recorded engagement ends faster than the
sensors can characterize it.

### COMBAT PROFILE — OBSERVED ABILITIES
- **Gravity-ripper greatblade.** Wields a single, exceptionally long katana-form
  blade that ripples the gravitational field along its edge — recorded parting
  armour plate, vehicles, and bedrock with one stroke.
- **Overcharged strikes.** Observations show a faint blue energy ghosting his
  attacks, suggesting an internal power source beyond the weapon itself.
- **Overwhelming control.** Fights with minimal motion and maximal result — no
  flourish, no wasted step, no opening offered.
- **Pairing.** Functions in perfect tandem with SPECIMEN VI ("Six"); the two
  cover each other's lines as if rehearsed for years.

### EQUIPMENT
One blade. He has not been observed to need a second weapon, or a first word.

### FIELD NOTES
Origin: **unknown.** Affiliation beyond the Dark Moon Six: **unknown.** Identity:
**unknown.** The Division's instruments can measure his power and cannot account
for it. Every attempt to build a fuller profile has returned the same result:
insufficient data, surviving observers few.

### CONTAINMENT RECOMMENDATION
None viable. Avoid. If encountered, do not initiate; withdraw and report.
