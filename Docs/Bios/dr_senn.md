> **CLASSIFICATION:** Company personnel (senior researcher)
> **ROLE:** Augment design, asset study, applied biotech
> **DISPOSITION:** Active, company-employed
> **PRESENTATION:** Tired, precise, increasingly uneasy with the work

### FUNCTION
The hands behind the augments hunters lease and the science the company sells as
safety. Senn knows the technical truth of what Scematica builds better than
anyone on the floor — and carries the weight of knowing it.

### BEHAVIOURAL NOTES
Speaks in careful, qualified sentences. The kind of expert who answers the
question you asked and quietly flinches at the one you didn't. Conscience present;
authority absent.

### FIELD RELEVANCE
A secondary voice in the contractor's orbit — explains the *how* of the company's
technology, when the company allows an explanation at all.
