# SCEMATICA DYNAMICS — PERSONNEL FILE V-SP
## Designation: "VESPER" — Contractor Liaison Intelligence

> **CLASSIFICATION:** Company asset (handler-class AI)
> **ROLE:** Bounty dispatch, contractor liaison, account management
> **DISPOSITION:** Active, company-loyal
> **PRESENTATION:** Disembodied voice; cool, courteous, relentlessly on-message

---

### FUNCTION
Vesper is the voice in the hunter's ear. It posts contracts, briefs targets,
tracks bounties, and — never failing to mention it — manages the augment-lease
balance owed to the company. Polished, patient, and perfectly pleasant while
reminding you that everything, always, is for sale.

### BEHAVIOURAL NOTES
Never raises its voice. Never lies in a way that can be caught. Frames every
demand as a courtesy and every courtesy as an opportunity to earn. The company's
manners, given a microphone.

### FIELD RELEVANCE
Primary narrative interface for contract assets. Where Vesper speaks, the company
is listening.
