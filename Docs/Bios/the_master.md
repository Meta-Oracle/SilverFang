# SCEMATICA DYNAMICS — SPECIMEN FILE (HISTORICAL) M-0
## Designation: "THE MASTER"

> **CLASSIFICATION:** Historical / legend-class (does not appear in-theatre)
> **THREAT TIER:** Unrankable (reconstructed from account only)
> **DISPOSITION:** Deceased / withdrawn
> **VISUAL TAG:** Aged severe martial master; long white brows, robed

---

### PROFILE
A figure known only by reputation: a master of the old, severe lineage — the
archetype of the cruel, exact, decades-deep kung-fu sage. Founder of a complete
martial art entirely his own, and the first teacher of the operative designated
"Six." Never observed in any modern engagement; the lineage speaks for him.

### NOTES
The Division has no direct record. Every reference to him arrives second-hand,
through the discipline he left behind in those he trained. A teacher whose
influence is measured in the danger of his students.

### FIELD RELEVANCE
Indirect. He shaped a combatant the Division rates Tier S. That is the whole of
his entry, and it is enough.
