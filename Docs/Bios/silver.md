# SCEMATICA DYNAMICS — SPECIMEN FILE S-1L
## Designation: "SILVER FANG"

> **CLASSIFICATION:** Independent contractor / apex bounty asset
> **THREAT TIER:** S (lethal to standard and elite response units alike)
> **DISPOSITION:** Active, unaligned, mercenary
> **VISUAL TAG:** Short silver hair, dark combat dress, sidearm + blade

---

### PHYSICAL PROFILE
Lean, fast, durable beyond baseline. Moves with trained economy — no wasted
motion, every step already loading the next strike. Reads the field constantly.

### COMBAT PROFILE — OBSERVED ABILITIES
- **Dual-stance combatant.** Switches fluidly between a **sword stance** (rapid
  light strings, heavy launchers, spinning AOE finishers) and a **gun stance**
  (semi-automatic revolver, one shot per pull, mag of six).
- **Ammunition attributes.** Rounds observed in multiple damage types — kinetic
  (white), nuclear (green, hardest-hitting), incendiary (orange), ice (cyan),
  and piercing. Impact colour reflects the round.
- **Charge system.** Holds attacks to release scaling charged variants (light,
  heavy, and a tiered charged revolver shot).
- **Awakened state.** Enters an overcharged blue form that converts every round
  into a psychic-class variant and unlocks blink-teleport strikes.
- **Mobility.** Dash with i-frames, sprint, sprint-jump that carries momentum,
  air combos, hover after air attacks, lock-on backpedal.
- **Combo ladder.** Sustains extended chains (F → XXX) that escalate payout and
  on-screen feedback the longer they hold.

### EQUIPMENT
High-cycle revolver; a balanced one-handed blade; leased cybernetic augments.

### FIELD NOTES
Treats the contract as the only law that applies to him. Sardonic, unhurried,
and far more dangerous than his casual manner suggests. Does not flinch at
yields the company designs to be unflinchable.

### CONTAINMENT RECOMMENDATION
Do not engage with standard units. Do not corner. Subject's value to the company
is exceeded only by the cost of trying to collect it.
