# SCEMATICA DYNAMICS — STATE OF THE WORLD
### Public Situation Report · Issued at Contract Open

*The official telling, as the Corporation tells it. This briefing carries only
what is true and known at the moment play begins — the board as it is set, with
none of the moves yet made. The company never lies. It simply never volunteers.*

---

## THE WORLD, IN ONE LINE
A world that runs on **SCEMA**, kept safe by **Scematica Dynamics**, paid for in
**fear** — and culled, contract by contract, by the hunters the company hires.

## THE CURRENCY
**SCEMA** is the lifeblood of the modern economy: the unit everything is priced
in, from a meal to a municipal wall to a leased cybernetic arm. SCEMA is earned,
above all, by **clearing bounties.** Value flows to those who make the streets
feel safer — and the streets, lately, need a great deal of making-safe.

## THE COMPANY
**Scematica Dynamics** is the institution at the centre of it all. It issues the
bounties. It designs and leases the augments hunters fight with. It sets policy,
shapes the messaging, and underwrites the stability the public depends on.
Officially, it is the reason the lights stay on. Its executive voice is
**Chairman Voss**; its research is led by figures such as **Dr. Senn**; its
contractors are managed by the liaison intelligence **Vesper.**

## THE YIELDS
The threat the bounties answer are the creatures the public calls monsters —
werewolves, titans, chimerae, drones, and worse. They appear in the districts;
they endanger civilians; hunters are dispatched to put them down for SCEMA. The
company brands them in myth because myth moves faster than fact, and a frightened
district buys more walls. (Internally they are catalogued; see the bounty
bestiary.)

## THE HUNTER
You begin as one of the company's apex contractors: an independent killer of
yields, paid in SCEMA, owing the company for the augments that keep you ahead of
what you hunt. The contracts are generous. The hours are bad. The work is steady,
because the monsters are.

## THE CITY, RIGHT NOW
At contract-open the arrangement holds: monsters appear, hunters answer, SCEMA
flows, the company prospers, the public sleeps behind walls it rents. It is, on
the surface, a stable world — a clean loop of threat and reward that has made
Scematica Dynamics indispensable and made fear the most reliable currency of all.

It is a very good arrangement.

*For someone.*
